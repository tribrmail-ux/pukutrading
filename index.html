The agent model

Why go through an agent instead of a distributor?

A distributor sells you what is in their warehouse. An agent goes and finds what fits your job. Those are different incentives, and it shows up on your invoice.

  • Nothing sits in a warehouse on your account A distributor's price carries the cost of stock standing still — the capital, the space, the product that expired before it sold. We buy against your order, so you are not paying rent on someone else's shelf.
  • Not tied to one supplier We hold no agency for any manufacturer. For a given enquiry we go to whoever has the right product available. If another route is cheaper or faster, we take it.
  • One contact for a load with many parts A roof needs sheets, purlins, screws, closures and flashings, often from different sources. You send one enquiry and get one quotation, one delivery and one invoice.
  • The east is not an afterthought Gobabis sits on the road between South African supply and central Namibian demand. Sites in Omaheke that Windhoek suppliers reach when it suits them are on our doorstep.
  • You see what you are buying Quotations state the product, the grade or profile, the packaging and the quantity. If a substitute is the sensible option, we say so and tell you what changes.

Chlorine strength verification

You are buying 12.5 % sodium hypochlorite. Are you dosing 12.5 %?

Hypochlorite decays from the day it is made — with heat, with time, with light. A drum that left the plant at label strength can arrive materially weaker, and nothing on the outside of it will tell you. We will titrate a sample of your existing stock, in front of you, and give you the available chlorine figure in writing. Whoever you buy from.
