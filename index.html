How it works

From enquiry to supply

Three steps, and a written quotation before anything is ordered.

    Tell us what you need

About Puku Trading

Owner-managed. Direct contact.

Puku Trading was established to make industrial sourcing simpler. Instead of spending time trying to identify suppliers across the region, you deal with a local commercial point of contact who helps define the requirement, approaches suitable suppliers, obtains quotations and coordinates the order process.

You deal directly with the person responsible for your enquiry, from the initial request through quotation and supply coordination. Where specialist product knowledge is required, we work with the relevant manufacturer or technical supplier rather than claiming that expertise sits in‑house.
