How it works

Nothing is ordered until you have approved a written quotation.

  1. Tell us what you need

    A part number, a specification, or a photograph of the old drum.

  2. We source and quote

    We go to the market and come back with a written price.

  3. You approve

    Nothing is ordered before you say so.

  4. We deliver

    We manage the order, the paperwork and the transport to your site.

An agent, not a distributor

Puku Trading Trust holds no stock and represents no single manufacturer. We buy against your order, from whoever is best placed to supply it, which keeps the cost of a warehouse off your invoice and leaves us free to go wherever the right product is.

Puku is one person. You deal with whoever handles your order, and we will tell you plainly when something is outside what we can do well.
