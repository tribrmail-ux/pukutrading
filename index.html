About Puku Trading

Owner-managed. Direct contact.

Puku Trading is a Namibia‑based, owner‑managed business providing technical drafting, specialised sourcing and practical business technology.

The business focuses on solving real operational problems without unnecessary complexity — whether that means preparing a roof sheeting layout, finding an industrial product or helping a small business implement a practical digital system.

Where specialist expertise is required, Puku works with appropriate suppliers and professionals rather than presenting expertise that does not sit in‑house.
