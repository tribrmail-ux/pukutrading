Roofing and building materials, delivered to site.

Sheeting is rolled to the length you order, so the measurement matters more than the price. Send us the spans and we will work back to a sheet list with you.

Sheeting

  • ProfilesIBR, corrugated, concealed‑fix and widespan
  • FinishesGalvanised, zinc‑aluminium coated, colour‑coated (Chromadek and equivalent), translucent sheets
  • LengthsRolled to order. Long single lengths avoid end laps — check what can reach the site first.
  • FlashingsRidges, barge and side flashings, closures, wall cladding to match the profile

Structure

  • Purlins and girtsLipped channel and Z‑purlins, galvanised or painted, cut to length
  • SectionsAngle, tube, channel, sheet and plate for supports and repairs
  • TrussesSteel and timber, quoted to a drawing or to a span and pitch
  • BracketsCleats, apex plates, tie‑rods, sag bars and knee braces

Fixings

  • Roofing screwsSelf‑drilling and self‑tapping, to timber or steel, with bonded washers and matching caps
  • RivetsSealed rivets, stitching screws, saddle washers
  • SealingProfile closures, foam fillers, butyl tape, sealants and flashing tape
  • AnchorsChemical and mechanical anchors, hold‑down bolts

Insulation, ceilings and rainwater

  • InsulationGlass wool and polyester blanket, foil‑faced sarking, board insulation, spacer systems
  • CeilingsGypsum and fibre‑cement board, PVC panels, cornice, brandering, grid and tiles
  • RainwaterGutter, downpipes, brackets, outlets and rainwater goods
  • Site consumablesDamp‑proof course and membranes, silicone and PU sealants, safety mesh

Quoting a roof

Send what you have. We will ask about the rest before anything is cut.

  • Spans and pitchRidge to eave, number of bays, pitch or rise, and any overhang
  • Profile and finishWhat is already there, if you are matching it. A photograph usually answers this.
  • Fixing into whatTimber or steel purlins — it changes every screw on the list
  • AccessWhat can reach the site, and what is there to offload with
  • ColourSheet, flashings, screw caps and gutter should be ordered together so they match
