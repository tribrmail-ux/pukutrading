Division 02Building & roofing materials

Roofing and building materials, cut to your job and delivered to site.

Sheeting, structure, fixings and finishing — quoted as one order.

Roof sheeting is usually rolled to the length you order, which makes the measurement more important than the price. Send us the spans and the pitch and we will work back to a sheet list with you before anything is ordered.

Sheeting

Roof and wall sheeting

  • ProfilesIBR · corrugated · concealed‑fix and standing seam · widespan and box profiles
  • FinishesGalvanised · zinc‑aluminium coated · colour‑coated (Chromadek and equivalent coated steel) · polycarbonate and fibreglass translucent sheets
  • Gauges and lengthsStandard gauges, rolled to length. Long single lengths avoid end laps, but check what can be turned into your site.
  • CladdingWall cladding, flashings, ridges, barge and side flashings to match the profile

Structure

Purlins, trusses and steelwork

  • Purlins and girtsLipped channel and Z‑purlins, galvanised or painted, cut to length
  • SectionsAngle, square and rectangular tube, channel, sheet and plate for supports and repairs
  • TrussesSteel and timber roof trusses quoted to a drawing or a span and pitch
  • Brackets and cleatsCleats, apex plates, tie‑rods, sag bars and knee braces

Fixings

Fasteners and closures

  • Roofing screwsSelf‑drilling and self‑tapping, to timber or steel, with bonded washers and colour caps to match the sheet
  • Rivets and stitchingSealed rivets, stitching screws, saddle washers
  • Closures and sealingProfile closures, foam fillers, butyl tape, sealants and flashing tape
  • AnchorsChemical and mechanical anchors, hold‑down bolts, roof‑bolt sets

Envelope

Insulation, ceilings and rainwater

  • Thermal insulationGlass wool and polyester blanket, foil‑faced sarking, board insulation, spacer systems for under‑sheet installation
  • CeilingsGypsum board, fibre‑cement board, PVC ceiling panels, cornice, brandering, suspension grid and tiles
  • RainwaterSeamless and jointed gutter, downpipes, brackets, outlets, leaf guard and rainwater goods
  • Site consumablesDamp‑proof course and membranes, silicone and PU sealants, fixing accessories, safety mesh

Ordering

What we need from you to quote a roof

You do not need all of it. Send what you have and we will ask about the rest before anything is cut.

  • Spans and pitchRafter length from ridge to eave, the number of bays, and the pitch or the rise. Include overhangs.
  • Profile and finishWhat is already there, if you are matching it. A photograph of the existing roof usually answers this.
  • Fixing into whatTimber or steel purlins — it changes every screw on the list.
  • AccessWhat can reach the site and what is available to offload. Long sheets and a narrow farm track are a bad combination.
  • ColourColour‑coated sheet, flashings, screw caps and gutter should be ordered together so they match.

We quote materials. We are not a building contractor and do not install — if you need someone on the roof, we will say so rather than pretend otherwise.

Delivery

Getting materials onto a site in the east

Roof sheets are long, light and easy to damage, and a lot of Namibian building happens a long way from a hardware counter. We plan the load with the site in mind: what the road can take, what is there to offload with, and whether the whole order should arrive at once or in the order the job needs it.
