AboutRegistered Namibian trust

Small, independent, and on the right road.

Puku Trading Trust is a sourcing and trading agent based in Gobabis.

We are new. We would rather say so than invent a history — you can judge us on how we handle your first enquiry.

What we are

An agent, not a distributor

Puku Trading Trust is a registered Namibian trust (registration [[TRUST REG NO]]) operating as an independent sourcing and trading agent. We do not manufacture, we do not hold stock, and we do not represent any single supplier.

What we do is stand between a Namibian buyer and a market that is mostly in another country: finding the product, getting it specified correctly, obtaining competitive pricing, placing and following the order, and making sure it arrives where it is supposed to. Our customers are mines, industrial plants, food and beverage processors, municipalities, farms and contractors — anyone who needs something that is not sitting in a shop down the road.

Where we are

Gobabis, on the corridor

The Trans‑Kalahari Corridor is the road link between South African industry and central Namibia, and Gobabis sits on it — roughly 205 km east of Windhoek and 110 km from the Botswana border at Buitepos. Goods coming north and west pass us before they reach the capital.

It also means the east is home ground. Omaheke — the farms, the towns, the sites out on the district roads — is a place most Windhoek suppliers service when a load happens to justify the trip. For us it is where we are, and a delivery to a farm past Witvlei or Leonardville is an ordinary week rather than a special arrangement.

How we operate

Deliberately small

Puku is one person. That is not an apology — it is the reason you can send a photograph at seven in the evening and get an answer from the person who will actually handle your order. There is no call centre, no ticket number and nobody to hand you on to.

It also sets honest limits. We are not going to pretend to a warehouse, a fleet or a technical department we do not have. Where something is outside what we can do properly, we will tell you and, where we can, point you at someone who can.

Principles

What you can hold us to

  • We quote what we can supplyIf we cannot source it at a price that makes sense, we say so rather than take the order and hope.
  • Substitutes are named as substitutesYou will never be sent an equivalent product without being told it is an equivalent.
  • The verification is free and unconditionalWe test your chlorine whether or not you buy from us, and you get the result in writing either way.
  • Problems come back to usOnce you have approved an order, chasing the supplier is our job, not yours.
  • We do not claim what we do not haveNo certifications we have not earned, no partnerships we do not hold, no experience we have not accumulated.

The name

Puku

A short name, easy to say in any of the languages spoken in this part of the country, and easy to remember on a delivery note. That is the whole story. We will not pretend it is a philosophy.
