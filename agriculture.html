Water

Borehole, reservoir and drinking water

Most farm water problems here are the same three: it is hard, it is brackish, or something is growing in the reservoir. All three are treatable once you know which one you have.

  • DisinfectionSodium and calcium hypochlorite, stabilised chlorine and dosing consumables for reservoirs and drinking troughs
  • Algae and biofilmReservoir and trough treatment, tank cleaning chemistry
  • Scale and hardnessAntiscalants and softening media for pumps, pipes and troughs
  • TestingSimple test kits and reagents so you can check the water yourself
  • ReticulationPipe, fittings, float valves, dosing pumps and tank accessories

If you chlorinate a reservoir, we will test what is actually in your drum, free →

Practical

How to deal with us if you farm

  • WhatsApp is fineA voice note and a photo of the empty bag is a perfectly good order enquiry. You do not need to write a specification.
  • Combine your loadsSalt, fencing wire and a drum of chlorine on one delivery beats three trips. Tell us everything you need, even the small items.
  • Say where the farm isDistrict and road, not just the postal town. It determines how the load gets to you and what it costs.
  • Ask before you need itSourced goods take time to get here. A week of notice usually costs less than an urgent load.
