Chlorine strength verification

You buy sodium hypochlorite by strength and dose it by strength. It is very rarely checked.

Hypochlorite begins losing available chlorine the day it is made, and heat and time accelerate it. A load that left the factory at label strength does not necessarily arrive at label strength, and nothing on the outside of a drum tells you what is inside it.

On the label
In the drum

An illustration of the problem, not a measurement. Your figure is whatever the titration gives on the day.

What we do

We come to your site and run an iodometric titration on a sample drawn from your own stock, in front of you. It is the standard method for available chlorine and it takes minutes.

You get the result in writing, against the strength you were invoiced for, with the date and the batch reference. Use it with your current supplier if you want to — we will run the test whether or not you ever buy from us.

A field titration on your samples. Not a laboratory certificate, and we do not present it as one.
